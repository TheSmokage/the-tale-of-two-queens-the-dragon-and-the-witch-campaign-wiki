## Summary

The party continued across the grasslands toward [[Greenest]] following their recent encounter with [[The Cult of Tiamat]]. Upon arriving, they found a modestly sized town surrounded by a wooden palisade. A keep within the town flew two banners: one blue and silver bearing the symbol of [[The Harpers]], and another depicting a crown on a field of red and yellow associated with [[The Lord's Alliance]].

Although [[Greenest]] appeared busy and lively, several details immediately seemed strange. Birds circled above the town but refused to land, and a strange sound could be heard emanating from within the settlement and carrying an unusual distance. The source of the sound remained unclear. In the distance, magical lights marked the location of the [[Afterlife Casino]], which was situated some distance outside of town.

The party went to the [[Bryne Widow Tavern]], where they met [[Verity Kye]], their [[The Golden Vault|Golden Vault]] contact. [[Verity Kye|Verity]] swore the party to absolute secrecy before explaining their assignment.

[[Verity Kye|Verity]] revealed that [[Quentin Togglepocket]]'s [[Afterlife Casino]] is themed around the Nine Hells and was constructed using money he had stolen from her. She wanted the party to rob [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] as an act of revenge, with three primary goals: steal the Erynyes Statuette, recover the 5,000 gold pieces stolen from her, and, most importantly, rob [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] of his dignity.

[[Verity Kye|Verity]] provided the party with a rough map of the casino, including its vault and known magical defenses. The map had apparently been created by a construction worker she knew named Gildur Drak. The party learned that the casino was protected by magical mirrors that appeared to monitor the premises, magical barriers restricting access to certain areas, and numerous physical guards and employees.

[[Verity Kye|Verity]] also explained that the casino could only be reached by boat after traveling some distance outside of [[Greenest]] toward the magical lights. The [[Afterlife Casino]] operates 24 hours a day. The Erynyes statuette was currently being displayed openly as the prize for a Three-Dragon Ante tournament that would conclude within the next day or so.

[[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] was described as a suave, refined, smooth-talking gnome who was an experienced Three-Dragon Ante player. He was known to host tournaments for his own enjoyment and prestige and was prone to making wagers. Despite his refined nature, he was also a quiet and extremely proud man.

Verity also provided information about the eight original tournament participants: Anya Belefleur, Karn Ironpebble, Ladia Misream, Whip Walsh, Jeda Moor, Nightshade, Ruthy Swiford, and Whumpus Thistledown. All of the casino's employees appeared to be tieflings.

Theo was particularly enthusiastic about the opportunity to get revenge against the dragon cultists, despite having little evidence that [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] was actually a member or leader of the cult. Verity provided him with a watered-down dose of Pale Tincture to use as he saw fit.

The party departed [[Greenest]] in a carriage and traveled to the casino's dock. From there, they boarded a boat that carried them into a cavernous chamber decorated to resemble the Nine Hells: the [[Afterlife Casino]]. The casino's overall theme centered around temptation. The boat departed whenever it was full or every fifteen minutes, whichever came first.

A strange enchanted river flowed through the center of the casino. Its appearance was reminiscent of the River Styx, filled with ghostly, soul-like forms. On a dare, Thwump touched the water and experienced a mysterious vision, though the nature of the vision was left to him.

Once inside, the party split up to cover as much of the casino as possible while gathering information. Each member carried a portion of the gold recovered from the cultist caravan, giving them at least 500 gp each to use during their investigation.

Theo went to the casino's restaurant, Nessus, which was located in a cave below the main floor beside a waterfall. He initially intended to find information, locate [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]], or discover a source of food that could potentially be poisoned. After determining that the kitchen appeared to serve the general public rather than cultists, Theo decided to make an extravagant display of his wealth instead. He spent freely while loudly demanding better food and wine, though he discovered that the restaurant possessed a special reserve accessible only to a select few, apparently at [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]]'s request. Theo immediately became interested in finding this private cache so that he could poison [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] without accidentally killing casino patrons.

Alice explored the casino's spa and discovered that employees used keycards to pass through the casino's magical security barriers. After causing a minor commotion by spilling her gold coins and drawing the attention of casino staff, Alice successfully stole one of the keycards.

While investigating the area beyond the spa, Alice noticed something unusual: many of the uniforms were sized for gnomes or halflings rather than tieflings. This suggested that the casino's magical security system or some other enchantment might allow ordinary gnomes, halflings, humans, and others to disguise themselves as tieflings, reinforcing the casino's Nine Hells theme.

Thwump took a trip to the spa with his dire wolf before deciding to attempt to enter the Three-Dragon Ante tournament. Carrying 1,000 gp, he managed to secure a place in the tournament and began playing with the intention of winning the Erynyes statuette legitimately.

Jila spent time on the main casino floor examining the slot machines and their infernal runes. Her investigation revealed something strange: the machines smelled of flowers, vegetables, and earth. Whatever was powering or influencing the machines might not have been quite what it appeared to be.

Jila then positioned herself in the main room and observed the casino's patrons until the party could reconvene. While doing so, she spotted [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] crossing the room just as Theo returned from his restaurant investigation.

After finally seeing [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]], Theo immediately formed another questionable plan. He approached the gnome angrily and spoke to him as though [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] were a servant, using the interaction as an excuse to engage him. Once [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] identified himself, Theo quickly changed tactics and began fawning over him, appealing to [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]]'s pride and aloof nature.

Theo successfully talked [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] into ordering drinks from his private reserve so that the two could share a toast. When the drinks arrived, Theo used sleight of hand to poison both glasses, ensuring that [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]]'s drink was poisoned while also poisoning his own as a precaution.

Both Theo and [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] drank their poisoned beverages. Each successfully resisted the initial effects of the poison, but both became poisoned for approximately a week. Theo took considerable delight in the fact that he had successfully poisoned [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] without immediately killing him.

As Thwump continued competing in the Three-Dragon Ante tournament, Alice, Jila, Theo, [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]], and many other spectators eventually gathered in the tournament room to watch the final rounds. Theo entered alongside [[Quentin Togglepocket|Quentin]] while continuing to fawn over him, repeatedly glancing toward Jila and Alice as they followed behind.

At this point, it became increasingly apparent that Theo might have another bad idea.
