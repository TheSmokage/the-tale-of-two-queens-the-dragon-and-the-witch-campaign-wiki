Small village housing the magically concealed [[The Golden Vault|Golden Vault]] safehouse hidden within what appears to be a dilapidated farmhouse. Despite its unassuming exterior, the interior is an expansive, highly secure headquarters belonging to the Golden Vault.

The safehouse itself is clad in opulent white marble. From the entrance, an inlaid silver path leads deeper into the building and opens into a large central chamber. From this chamber, numerous halls branch outward like the spokes of a wheel, providing access to the various facilities within the safehouse.

The halls contain vast bookshelves and numerous rooms, including:

- A large golden vault containing more than simple treasure. The contents include coinage, creatures, and individuals held within its secured chambers.
- An initiation room, used for [[The Golden Vault|Golden Vault]] operations and new members.
- Barracks and utility rooms supporting the agents who operate from the safehouse.
- A library, containing information on subjects relevant to [[The Golden Vault]] and the wider world.

A massive [[World Map]] occupies one wall of the central chamber, serving as a strategic reference for [[The Golden Vault]]. The map identifies numerous locations of interest and other Golden Vault safehouses, giving agents potential leads for future investigations and operations.

The safehouse also contains a wealth of information that can be discovered by exploring its rooms and library. Among the discoveries are references to [[The Twins]], two prominent mountains:

- [[Mt. Stonewick]]
- [[Silver Dragon Mountain]]

The library also contains information revealing that the broaches bear the symbol of [[Tiamat]], the godlike five-headed dragon who attempted to conquer the world centuries ago. The symbol's presence suggests that [[Tiamat]] has once again attracted the worship of a [[The Cult of Tiamat|cult]], providing an early indication of the larger threat surrounding the campaign.

The safehouse is also a place where the party can resupply and prepare for future missions. Alice discovered high-quality rations suitable for a noble and stocked the party's Bag of Holding with them for their travels.
