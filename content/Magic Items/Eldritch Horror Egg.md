### Murkmire Stone — Pulse & Effects

**Pulse**

- Every **10 minutes**
- Creatures within **20 ft.**: **DC 10 Wisdom save**
- Creature holding the stone: **disadvantage**
- Failed save: **roll 1d12**
- A new effect **replaces the previous effect**

| d12    | Effect                                                        |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**  | Vulnerability to psychic damage; can't maintain concentration |
| **2**  | Advantage on Strength checks & saves                          |
| **3**  | Disadvantage on Dexterity checks & saves                      |
| **4**  | Resistance to piercing & slashing damage                      |
| **5**  | Walking speed +5 ft.                                          |
| **6**  | Disadvantage on attack rolls                                  |
| **7**  | Disadvantage on Charisma checks & saves                       |
| **8**  | Disadvantage on Dexterity checks & saves                      |
| **9**  | Attack rolls against you have disadvantage                    |
| **10** | Disadvantage on Intelligence checks & saves                   |
| **11** | Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks                    |
| **12** | +2 AC                                                         |
Hatches into [[Eldritch Horror]].
may or may not react with [[Fey Liquid]].
