
The golden vault is a secret organization associated with [[Metallic Dragons]].
Missions are given by secret key and key goes to music box.
# ##Initiation Requirements
1. Trial Heist
2. Each Crew Must have a handler
3. Has tiers
## Golden Vault Oath

1. “Protect the innocent.”
2. “Preserve the balance.”
3. “Turn shadows toward the light.”
4. “Never steal for yourself what must be guarded for all.”
5. “Do good no matter the cost.”
## Golden Vault Badge
- Tier 1
	- **Sending:** 1/Long Rest communication between the party and their handler.
	- **Detect Magic:** 1/Long Rest, shared across the group.
	- **Safehouse Access:** Allows entry into Golden Vault safehouses.    
	- **Objective Compass:** Provides guidance toward relevant objectives.
